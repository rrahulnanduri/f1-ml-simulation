Suspension, Brakes and Tyres

In plain English

Each wheel hangs from the car on a set of arms, the suspension, that let it move up and down over bumps. A spring and a damper stop it bouncing. A brake disc inside the wheel turns speed into heat. The tyre is the only part that touches the road.

This is one front wheel on a test rig, with the road shown as a pad under the tyre that you can push.

Try this first

  1. Press Hit the kerb. The wheel jumps, the rod and the rocker turn, and the damper soaks up the shock.
  2. Raise Speed to 300 km/h. Air pushing down on the car squeezes the suspension.
  3. Turn on See through the tyre, then hold the brake. The brake disc glows as it turns speed into heat.

Words used here

Suspension
The arms, spring and damper that connect the wheel to the car and let it move over bumps.
Damper
A piston in oil that resists movement. It stops the spring bouncing on and on.
Torsion bar
A steel bar that acts as a spring by twisting.
Kerb
The raised stripe at the edge of a corner that drivers cut across.
Downforce
Air pushing the car down onto the road as it goes faster.
Grip
How well the tyre holds onto the road. It has to be shared between braking and turning.
Caliper
The clamp that squeezes brake pads onto the disc.
Contact patch
The small area of tyre that is touching the road at any moment.
