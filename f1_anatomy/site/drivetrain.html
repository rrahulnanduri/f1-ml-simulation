Transmission and Steering

In plain English

The gearbox passes the engine's power to the wheels. Low gears give strong pull for pulling away; high gears give high speed. An F1 car has eight, and the driver changes them with two paddles behind the steering wheel.

The steering wheel is also the control panel. Its dials change how the brakes and the engine behave, and it pops off so the driver can climb in and out.

Try this first

  1. Press the upshift button. Watch the highlighted gear change as the little drum at the back turns and slides a ring.
  2. Turn Shift for me off and use the up and down arrow keys. The numbers on the wheel's screen change with you.
  3. Press Take the wheel off. The screen goes blank and the paddles stop working.

Try it

Upshifts near 14,000 rpm and downshifts when the engine drops under about 7,500.

Words used here

Gear ratio
How many times the engine turns for one turn of the wheels. A big ratio gives strong pull, a small one high speed.
Sequential
You can only go up or down one gear at a time, in order, with no skipping.
Paddle shifter
A lever behind the steering wheel that the driver pulls to change gear.
Dog ring
A ring that slides along a shaft and locks a free-spinning gear to it with interlocking teeth.
Brake balance
How the braking force is split between the front and rear wheels. The driver adjusts it with a dial.
Differential
Lets the two driven wheels turn at different speeds in a corner. Its setting changes how the car handles when accelerating out of corners.
Engine mode
A setting that changes how much power the engine and the electric motors give.
rpm
How many times the engine turns each minute.
