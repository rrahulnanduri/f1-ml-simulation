Aerodynamics

In plain English

Aerodynamics is how air flows around the car. The wings work like upside-down aeroplane wings: instead of lifting, they push the car down onto the road. More push down means more grip, so the car can take corners much faster.

The price is drag, the air pushing back. DRS is a flap that opens on straights to let air through, cutting drag so the car goes faster.

Try this first

  1. Raise the tunnel speed. The push-down force grows very quickly with speed.
  2. Click the rear wing, or press Space. The flap opens: drag drops and the top speed rises.
  3. Lower the ride height. The floor works harder, until the car is too low and the air stops flowing properly.

Words used here

Downforce
Air pushing the car down onto the road. It adds grip without adding weight.
Drag
Air pushing back against the car. It limits the top speed.
DRS
Drag Reduction System. The driver opens a flap on the rear wing on straights so less air resists the car.
Ride height
How far the floor of the car is above the track.
Stall
When the air under the floor stops flowing smoothly, the suction collapses and downforce drops sharply.
Porpoising
The car bounces up and down at speed as the floor alternately stalls and recovers.
kN (kilonewton)
A unit of force. 10 kN is about a tonne pushing down.
